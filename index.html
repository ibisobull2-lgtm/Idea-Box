Questions worth answering

1. Is the problem real?
Talk to potential customers before spending much money.
2. Will customers pay?
Ask what they currently use and what they would realistically pay.
3. Can each sale make money?
Remember transport, packaging, fees, marketing and other costs.
4. Can you test it small?
Try a low-cost pilot before making a large commitment.

Your data

Keep a backup of the information saved in this browser.

Your transactions, budget, and savings goal are stored locally in this browser. They are not synced to an online account.

Restore accepts a MoneyMind JSON backup. Import replaces the current transactions, budget, and savings goal, so export your current data first if you want to keep it.
A backup file may contain personal financial information. Keep it somewhere private. Clearing saved data cannot be undone unless you have a backup.
